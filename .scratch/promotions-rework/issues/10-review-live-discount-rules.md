# 10: Review the live Discount Rules before launch (owner task)

**What to build:** A short written list of the roughly 20 Discount Rules running on the live site, with a verdict for each: keep, replace by one of the new Deals or Member Privilege, or switch off. Also record whether the Points & Rewards plugin's membership levels are on. Nothing in this spec goes live until this is done, because the old rules could discount the same items again on top of the new ones.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] The live status of the Points & Rewards membership levels is recorded
